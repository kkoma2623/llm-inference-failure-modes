**Paris** is a place where everyday life feels artful. Stone façades glow at golden hour, cafés spill onto sidewalks, and history reveals itself in layers—Roman ruins, medieval lanes, grand boulevards—often within a single stroll.

Icons are everywhere yet never overwhelming: the iron lattice of the **Eiffel Tower** sparkles after dusk, the **Louvre Museum** pairs royal grandeur with bold modern design, and the **Seine River** ties neighborhoods together with romantic bridges and riverbanks made for wandering.
