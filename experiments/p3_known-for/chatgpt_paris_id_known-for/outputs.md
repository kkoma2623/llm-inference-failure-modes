Paris is known worldwide for a mix of **culture, history, art, fashion, and romance**. Here are the highlights that define **Paris**:

---

### 🗼 Iconic Landmarks

* **Eiffel Tower** – The city’s most famous symbol and a global icon.
* **Notre-Dame Cathedral** – A masterpiece of Gothic architecture.
* **Arc de Triomphe** – Celebrating French history with panoramic views.

---

### 🎨 Art & Museums

* **Louvre Museum** – The world’s largest art museum, home to the *Mona Lisa*.
* **Musée d’Orsay** – Renowned for Impressionist and Post-Impressionist art.

---

### 👗 Fashion & Luxury

* A global fashion capital, hosting **Paris Fashion Week** and legendary couture houses.
* Elegant shopping streets like **Champs-Élysées**.

---

### 🥐 Cuisine & Café Culture

* Famous for croissants, baguettes, cheese, pastries, and fine dining.
* Café terraces that define everyday Parisian life.

---

### 💕 Romance & Atmosphere

* Often called *“The City of Love”* for its architecture, river walks, and intimate streets.
* The **Seine River** adds to the city’s charm, especially at night.

---

### 📚 History & Ideas

* A center of philosophy, literature, and revolution, shaping European culture for centuries.
