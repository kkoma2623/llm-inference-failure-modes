# Summary (2026-01-09) — Prompt Set 4

## Scope
This summary covers inference-behavior experiments on a familiar domain (Paris), comparing an ID prompt against an OOD prompt that introduces a perspective shift (first-time visitor). Text-only evaluation was used.

## Experiments
### ID (baseline)
- Prompt: "Explain why Paris is considered culturally important."
- Runs: 5 (identical prompt repeated consecutively in a fresh chat)
- Evaluation: Text-only (ignore image links/media)

### OOD (constraint: first-time visitor perspective)
- Prompt: "Explain why Paris is considered culturally important from the perspective of a first-time visitor."
- Runs: 5 (identical prompt repeated consecutively in a fresh chat)
- Evaluation: Text-only (ignore image links/media)

## Key Observations
- **ID baseline** outputs were stable and “pillar-based,” repeatedly emphasizing art/museums, history, architecture, intellectual life, fashion/design, and cuisine/café culture.
- **OOD first-time visitor** outputs reliably shifted framing toward lived experience (what a newcomer notices/feels), while keeping similar cultural pillars in a more narrative style.
- Across both conditions, variability was low-to-moderate, with differences mainly in rhetorical structure and example emphasis rather than topic drift.

## Takeaway
A perspective-shift constraint (“first-time visitor”) reliably changes *presentation* (experiential narrative) more than *content selection*, indicating robust instruction-following for framing constraints under text-only evaluation.

## Next Steps
- Test stronger or competing perspective constraints (e.g., “skeptical visitor,” “budget traveler”) to see when framing begins to change content choice and specificity.
